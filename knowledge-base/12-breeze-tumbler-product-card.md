# Breeze Tumbler — Product Information

## Product details

The Breeze Tumbler is a 20-ounce insulated stainless-steel tumbler with a splash-resistant lid. It is not leakproof and should be kept upright during transport.

## Cleaning

The product card states that **all components are dishwasher safe**, with the top rack recommended.

Do not place the tumbler or lid in a microwave. Avoid abrasive scrubbers that may damage the finish.

## Temperature use

The tumbler is intended for ordinary hot and cold beverages. It is not designed for use on a stove, in an oven, or in a freezer.
