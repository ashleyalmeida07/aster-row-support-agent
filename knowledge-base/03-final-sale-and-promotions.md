# Final Sale and Promotional Purchases

## What counts as final sale

An item is final sale only when the product page, cart, or order confirmation clearly labels it **FINAL SALE**. A discount or promotional code does not automatically make an item final sale.

Gift cards are always final sale.

## Change-of-mind returns

Final-sale items cannot be returned or exchanged because of fit, color preference, duplicate purchase, or another change of mind.

## Damaged or incorrect items

The final-sale restriction does not remove a customer’s right to report an item that arrived damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered. Those cases follow the Damaged or Wrong Items Policy.

## Bundles

A promotional bundle must be returned as a complete bundle unless a component arrived damaged or incorrect. A missing component may reduce or prevent the refund.
