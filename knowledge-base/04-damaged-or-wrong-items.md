# Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Items

## Reporting window

Customers should report an item that arrived damaged, visibly defective, or different from what was ordered within **7 calendar days of delivery**.

The customer should provide the order ID, a short description, and clear photographs of the item and packaging when reasonably possible.

## Available resolutions

After review, Aster & Row may offer a replacement, refund, or another appropriate resolution. Availability of a replacement depends on stock.

A return shipping fee is not charged when Aster & Row confirms that the item arrived damaged or that the wrong item was sent.

## Final-sale items

Final-sale items are still eligible for review when they arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect. Final sale only prevents change-of-mind returns.

## Reports after seven days

A manufacturing defect reported after the seven-day arrival window may be considered under the Warranty Policy. Normal wear, accidental damage, and misuse are not arrival defects.

The support agent must not promise that a refund or replacement has been approved before a human review is completed.
