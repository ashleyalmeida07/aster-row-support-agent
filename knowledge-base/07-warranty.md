# Limited Product Warranty

## Warranty periods

- Aster & Row bags and backpacks: **2 years from the purchase date**.
- Drinkware: **1 year from the purchase date**.
- Packing cubes and other travel accessories: **1 year from the purchase date**.

Aster & Row does not offer a lifetime warranty.

## What is covered

The limited warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship under normal use. Examples may include a seam that separates without misuse or a zipper mechanism that fails because of a manufacturing defect.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover ordinary wear, cosmetic changes, accidental damage, airline or carrier damage, improper cleaning, modification, neglect, or misuse.

## Final-sale products

A product being marked final sale does not remove the limited warranty for a qualifying manufacturing defect.

## Review process

Warranty claims require proof of purchase and may require photographs. A human support specialist reviews eligibility. The agent may explain the policy and collect information, but it must not promise approval.
