# International Shipping

## Supported destinations

Aster & Row currently ships internationally only to **Canada**. Shipping to other countries is not available at this time.

## Canada delivery estimate

Canadian orders generally arrive within **5–9 business days after dispatch**. Processing time before dispatch is usually 1–2 business days.

Delivery estimates may be extended by customs processing or carrier delays.

## Duties and taxes

Import duties, taxes, and brokerage charges are not prepaid by Aster & Row. The recipient is responsible for charges assessed by Canadian authorities or the carrier.

## Canadian returns

Aster & Row does not provide prepaid labels for ordinary Canadian change-of-mind returns. The customer is responsible for return postage. When Aster & Row confirms that an item arrived damaged or incorrect, support will provide an appropriate resolution without charging an ordinary return fee.

Direct exchanges are not offered for Canadian orders. An eligible customer may return the item and place a new order.
