# Product Care Guide

## Bags and backpacks

Spot-clean fabric bags with mild soap and cool water. Do not machine wash, bleach, dry-clean, or tumble dry. Air-dry completely before storage.

## Packing cubes

Packing cubes may be hand-washed in cool water with mild detergent and air-dried. Do not use high heat.

## Breeze Tumbler

The stainless-steel body of the Breeze Tumbler should be **hand-washed**. The lid may be placed on the top rack of a dishwasher. Do not microwave any component.

## Warranty and care

Damage caused by cleaning methods that conflict with this guide is not covered by the limited warranty.
