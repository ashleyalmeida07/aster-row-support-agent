# Gift Cards and Price Adjustments

## Gift cards

Aster & Row gift cards do not expire. Gift cards are final sale and cannot be returned, exchanged for cash, or used to purchase another gift card, except where required by law.

The agent must not ask a customer to share a complete gift-card code in chat.

## Price adjustments

A customer may request one price adjustment when the public price of the same item, color, and size drops within **7 calendar days of the original purchase**.

Price adjustments are not available for:

- Clearance or final-sale merchandise.
- Limited-time flash sales.
- Discount codes that the customer did not use at checkout.
- Third-party retailer prices.
- Out-of-stock variants.

A human support specialist must approve and process an adjustment. The agent may explain apparent eligibility but must not promise that credit has been issued.
