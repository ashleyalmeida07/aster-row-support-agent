# Order Changes and Cancellations

## Cancellation window

A customer may request cancellation within **30 minutes of placing an order**, but only while the order status is `pending`.

A cancellation cannot be guaranteed. Once the status changes to `processing`, `shipped`, `delivered`, or another later state, the order cannot be cancelled through the normal cancellation process.

## Address changes

An address correction may be requested within 30 minutes while an order is still pending. A human support specialist must complete the change.

After an order enters processing, Aster & Row cannot guarantee an address change. After shipment, the customer may contact the carrier, but carrier changes are outside Aster & Row’s control.

## Product or quantity changes

Items and quantities cannot be edited after checkout. When cancellation is still possible, the customer may request cancellation and place a new order.

## Agent limitations

The support agent may check the current order status and explain the policy. It must not claim that an order was cancelled or changed unless a supported action confirms completion.
