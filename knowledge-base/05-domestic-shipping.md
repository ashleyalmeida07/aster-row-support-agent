# Domestic Shipping

## Processing time

Most orders require **1–2 business days** for processing before they are dispatched. Business days exclude weekends and United States federal holidays.

## Delivery estimates after dispatch

- Contiguous United States: **3–5 business days**.
- Alaska and Hawaii: **5–8 business days**.
- PO boxes: **5–9 business days**.

These are estimates rather than guaranteed delivery dates.

## Shipping charges

Standard shipping is free for eligible United States orders of **$75 or more after discounts and before tax**. TrailPlus members receive free standard domestic shipping without a minimum order value.

Expedited shipping charges are shown at checkout and are not refundable unless Aster & Row shipped the wrong item or the shipment was damaged in transit.

## Delivery problems

A shipment marked delivered but not received should be reported to support. The agent may explain the policy but must not claim that a carrier investigation has been opened unless the application actually supports that action.
